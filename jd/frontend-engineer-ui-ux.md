## About MonoEdge

MonoEdge builds an advanced intelligence layer for the Indian mid-market
manufacturing sector. Our software behaves like an always-on analyst rather than
another dashboard: it reads what is happening across a plant, connects
production data to quality, energy, and cost, and tells the people running the
floor what is going wrong and why — in English, Hindi, and Marathi.

We are a technically-driven, bootstrapped company defining a new category in
industrial optimization.

## About the role

The intelligence in our product is only as useful as the screen it arrives on.
Our user is a plant owner or a shift supervisor who has been shown dashboards
before and was disappointed — too many charts, not enough answers. The interface
is where we either earn their trust or lose it.

As a Front End Engineer, you will build the parts of the product people actually
look at: the reports, the alerts, and the views that take a hard industrial
problem and make it legible in a glance. This is a UI/UX-focused role — clarity
is the whole job. A screen that is beautiful but confusing has failed.

## What you will do

- **Build product UI** in React and TypeScript, from small components up to full
  views.
- **Design data-dense interfaces** — charts, tables, and reports that present
  real production data honestly, including when the data is uncertain or
  incomplete.
- **Own the UX for non-technical users** — the person using this is running a
  factory, not reading documentation. Flows have to be obvious on the first try.
- **Handle multilingual, on-the-floor design** — screens that hold up in
  English, Hindi, and Marathi (Devanagari) and stay readable on a tablet in a
  bright, busy plant.
- **Maintain a small design system** with the Founder and the designer — a
  consistent set of components and styles.
- **Sweat the details** — empty states, loading, errors, and the difference
  between "no data" and "zero".

## Who should apply

We are looking for a recent graduate in **any engineering discipline, Computer
Science, or Design** — or a self-taught developer with work to show. The degree
matters less than what you have built.

You are a strong fit if you have:

- Solid web fundamentals: **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, with real comfort in
  the browser. You can build a responsive layout by hand.
- **React** experience — at least one real interface you have built — and a
  willingness to work in **TypeScript**.
- A genuine eye for **UI/UX**: you notice spacing, alignment, and hierarchy, and
  you can explain why one layout reads more clearly than another.
- A preference for **clarity over cleverness** — you would rather a user
  understand a screen in one pass than admire an animation.
- Something to show: a personal, college, or freelance project you can walk us
  through and take responsibility for.

**Nice to have** (none required): any charting or data-visualisation work;
Figma comfort; Devanagari or multilingual layout experience; an eye for
performance; familiarity with Git and the command line.

> **A note on marks:** we do not screen on CGPA alone. A project you can explain,
> and a real eye for interface quality, count for more than a decimal point.

## What you will learn and why join

- Ownership of real product surfaces early, working directly with the **Founder
  and senior team**.
- How to build interfaces for people whose job is not software, and how to
  present data honestly.
- A small, bootstrapped team where your work reaches a real customer quickly.
